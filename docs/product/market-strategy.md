# Market and commercialization strategy

[中文](market-strategy_CN.md)

## Incumbent alternative

The primary competitor is not another startup: it is tolerating Hikvision's official client and smart playback. Nearby alternatives include Frigate/Blue Iris for enthusiasts, heavyweight VMS/cloud platforms, smart-camera replacement, door-lock/PMS logs and manual review.

## Wedge versus differentiation

The NVR-only Desktop is an integration wedge. It proves installation, device compatibility and clip delivery, and may be useful as a free/limited tool. It cannot sustainably claim better event signal because its data is largely the same NVR source.

The differentiated product begins when an independent, quality-gated timeline reduces review work while preserving important-event recall. Radar is the first technical source, not the only future source.

## Commercial path

```text
Free/limited NVR workflow
        ↓
Timeline-capable Pro license
        ↓
Certified radar/gateway companion
        ↓
Installer/channel bundle and support
```

Exact pricing, limits and subscriptions remain hypotheses until pilot evidence exists.

## Moat

- Evidence-backed Hikvision/model compatibility.
- Reliable time/channel-to-media resolution and export.
- Productized HA-free sensing, calibration and diagnostics.
- Quality policies validated against labelled operations data.
- Installer workflow, support evidence and distribution channels.

The moat is not another HA card or merely open-sourcing an interval schema.

## Open-source role

The existing mmWave projects remain a laboratory for protocol/model breadth, calibration and algorithm validation. A future open Engine could build technical trust and third-party producers, but licensing is unresolved. Open-source popularity is not a substitute for install success, clip reliability or paid operational value.

## Channel assumption

The founder does not build a nationwide installation workforce. After a repeatable product exists, local CCTV installers can deliver certified hardware/configuration. Product design must therefore include diagnostics and installation proof that can travel without the founder.
